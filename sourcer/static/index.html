    How the score works
    • Impact — stars on projects they own
    • Seed — commits to the role's flagship repos (the strongest signal)
    • Notable — other 1k+ star projects they contributed to this year
    • Activity — contributions in the last year and last 90 days
    • Experience — account age and followers
    • Fit — languages, topics, bio keywords and seniority words

    Treat the score as a pointer for your own judgement, never as an automatic reject. Contact people once, personally, and honour opt-outs.

    Connect GitHub

    The tool needs a read-only GitHub token to search public profiles.

    1. Open github.com/settings/personal-access-tokens/new
    2. Choose Public repositories (read-only). No other permissions are needed.
    3. Generate it, copy it and paste it below. It's saved to .env on this computer only.
